

# Rogue meta

After 80 hours of rogue i found 2 builds i like the most.

Both use the Thornlace in the arsenal. I like the healing skill and the passive.

You can use ==the ratsar dagger or the pick==.

The dagger is better for bosses without many ads or if you have spaced mob waves (you can use Urge to Kill to clear most waves), its also harder to play, but you can 100% get faster times if you don't die.

An example is the abyss dungeon, since you are mostly killing single target enemies i like using the dagger there, for the cheese station too. But the Elisabeth dungeon or the golcano i prefer using the pick.

Pick and Thornlace, with a good crit build, you can get close to 40% crit after all the buffs, so you are building shields like crazy (ive seen 600+ shields on my rogue). It deals less damage in single target (but still enough to clear any single target boss), its insane for AoE damage. And the sustain is crazy, you can facetank most things.

==For gear i like using crit and armor pen (rogue only pieces, focus on crit), the only piece that isnt rogue only is the chest (we should get one when they release more dungeons tho)==, i also use the fever pants since its the only rogue only pants. I can get like 32% armon pen, with the crafted neck and rings and everything enchanted properly.

Also around 390-400 HP.

So you can learn the bosses with the pick and once you can clear easily you can try switching to the dagger and clear faster.

I can very easily solo any hard dungeon/boss. The hardest one is the station rat cus its bugged as hell and sometimes you just die to a train.

Both weapons i use the ==flaming weapon enchant==, i tried the crit but i didnt like it.

For armor i usually go for the ==armor+vit enchants, with dex on the gloves and crit+armor pen on the neck/rings==.

Finally for talents i like the ==left side of the tree, only using the last 2 points in the middle to get 20% chance of applying poisons from weapon skills==.

# fervor vs crit vs flaming weapon

Fervor: This is a core stat that directly boosts your overall damage output. It increases your critical hit strength and scales your spell power, making it incredibly effective for both physical and magic classes against tough bosses.

Flaming (Weapon): This is a rare weapon enchantment that adds a flat amount of fire damage to your attacks and applies a burning effect over time. It is excellent for early-game leveling and against enemies weak to fire. However, its effectiveness falls off when facing enemies with high fire resistance or immunity.

Which should you choose? Choose Fervor if you are building for the endgame, as it provides consistent scaling and helps bypass enemy defenses.
