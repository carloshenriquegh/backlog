Fixing Excalidraw Color InversionOpen your Excalidraw drawing in Obsidian.
Access the Excalidraw plugin settings or toggle the menu options.
`appeareance and behavior` > `theme and styling`
Change Dynamic Styling from `Match color scheme` to ==OFF==, or switch it to grayscale/gray match tone to prevent colors from washing out or inverting.

# Fixing PDF Color Inversion (only work for plugins)

Click on the Display options (three dots or settings icon in the PDF reader view). Toggle off or adjust Adapt to theme if it is forcing an inversion of your document's original colors and diagrams.
